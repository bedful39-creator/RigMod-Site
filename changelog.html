How RigMod updates work

RigMod is updated in place — when a new version ships, the official download page always serves the newest universal JAR under its versioned filename (for example RigMod-v4.8-Universal.jar). Your old JAR can stay in the mods folder or be swapped out; the version numbering on this page doubles as the RigMod version history reference. Maintenance states and deprecations are announced here first, so bookmark this RigMod changelog and check back after every Minecraft update that touches loader mappings.
