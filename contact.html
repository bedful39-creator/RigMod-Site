Get in touch

Contact RigMod DonutSMP

Questions about installing the mod, a bug to report, or a partnership idea? Our support team is here for streamers and creators.

Support & installation help

Most installation questions are answered in the FAQ on our homepage. If your issue isn't covered there — for example a loader conflict or a crash on launch — email us and include your Minecraft version, loader (Fabric or Forge), and launcher.

Bug reports

Found a visual glitch or a rig that isn't rendering as expected? Send us a short clip or screenshot along with your mod version. Reports for the Blackjack chest GUI rig, paper game rig, and 50/50 coinflip rig are triaged first.

Partnerships & creators

Streaming to a DonutSMP audience and want to collaborate? We work with creators of every size. Mention your channel and typical audience size when you write.

Email: support@rigmod.net

Response time: usually within 24–48 hours.
