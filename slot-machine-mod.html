DonutSMP Slot Machine Mod — Rigged Slots, Client-Side

RigMod is the DonutSMP slot machine mod that rigs slot machines, gambling machines, and casino machines entirely client-side — your reels hit jackpots on stream while the server's machine runs untouched underneath.

What the slot machine rig does

Open any DonutSMP slot machine with RigMod installed and the client-side engine takes over what you see: reels roll, then resolve to the display you want — jackpot, big win, or specific symbol line. It works across every DonutSMP gambling machine variant: classic slots, custom player-built casino machines, and dispenser-based contraptions. The rigged slots are visuals only — no packets leave your client, which is why it stays stream-proof and the machine's actual behavior is unchanged.

How slot machine rigging works on DonutSMP

  • Client-side intercept: RigMod hooks the visual render layer of the machine's GUI — not the server logic.
  • Outcome display swap: whatever the machine rolls, your screen shows the winning arrangement you selected.
  • Chat & audio cues: win messages and sound feedback render client-side to match the jackpot visuals.
  • Universal coverage: one gambling machine mod covers slots, Blackjack, coinflips, and paper games — see the full feature list.

RigMod vs other gambling machine tools

Most Minecraft slot machine mods are server plugins — they need install rights on the server and change gameplay for everyone. RigMod is a client: nothing to install server-side, nothing visible to other players, and per-session control from your own Minecraft window. That's what makes it the DonutSMP slots mod of choice for streaming: you rig your visuals, the server's logs stay clean, and your audience sees a seamless casino client experience.

Stream-proof rigged slots in OBS

The RigMod gambling client renders inside your Minecraft engine, so OBS, Streamlabs, and Discord game capture pick up the rigged reels natively — no overlays, no external windows, no mod menu flashes. Set your capture once and every rigged spin records like vanilla gameplay.
