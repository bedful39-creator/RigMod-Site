Is RigMod stream-proof on OBS & Discord? +

Yes. RigMod renders visual outcomes directly within your Minecraft client engine, making all spoofed wins, cards, and outcome messages appear seamless on OBS, Streamlabs, and Discord screen capture with zero visible mod menus.

Which Minecraft versions and mod loaders are supported? +

RigMod Universal supports Minecraft Java versions 1.20.1, 1.20.2, 1.20.4, 1.21, and 1.21.4+ across both Fabric and Forge mod loaders.

How do I install RigMod? +

Simply download the universal .jar file from the download button, drop it inside your .minecraft/mods folder, and launch your preferred Fabric or Forge profile in your Minecraft launcher.

Is RigMod free to download? +

Yes. RigMod Universal is completely free to download — one universal .jar file works with both Fabric and Forge. There are no subscriptions, licenses, or paywalled features.

Does RigMod affect other players or the server? +

No. RigMod is 100% client-side: every visual change happens only on your own screen. The DonutSMP server and other players never receive modified data, which is what makes the visuals stream-proof.
