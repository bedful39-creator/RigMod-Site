About the project

About RigMod DonutSMP

RigMod is a 100% client-side visual rig mod built for DonutSMP streamers and content creators who want full control over what their audience sees.

Who builds RigMod?

RigMod is developed by a small team of Minecraft modders and DonutSMP content creators. We started as streamers ourselves and got tired of the guesswork around Blackjack, paper games, and 50/50 coinflip content — so we built a client-side visual mod that gives creators a predictable, stream-ready outcome display every single time.

How does the visual rig work?

RigMod renders its changes entirely on your own Minecraft client. The DonutSMP server and other players never receive modified data — only what appears on your screen changes. That client-first design is why the visuals stay seamless in OBS, Streamlabs, and Discord game capture with no visible menus or HUD artifacts.

What can you rig?

  • Blackjack chest GUI rig — visually replace dealer cards, player hands, and outcome messages.
  • Paper game & dispenser rig — display winning renamed paper items and victory chat messages.
  • 50/50 coinflip rig — render a seamless visual win for coinflips and duels.

Compatibility

RigMod Universal ships as a single .jar file supporting Fabric and Forge on Minecraft Java 1.20.1 through 1.21.4+. One download covers every setup — no separate builds, no subscriptions. Head over to the homepage to download the mod, or read the FAQ for installation details.
